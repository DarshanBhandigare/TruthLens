# TruthLens AI — Multilingual Fact Verification Platform

A polished, humanized frontend prototype for an AI-powered multilingual fact verification platform, designed for hackathons and demonstration showcases.

## 🎯 What is TruthLens?

TruthLens helps citizens and journalists verify suspicious messages, forwarded news, images, and viral claims with clear, explainable breakdowns and source guidance in 8 regional Indian languages.

> **Tagline:** "Pause. Verify. Share Responsibly."

---

## 🚀 Key Prototype Features

- **No Generic AI Clutter / Humanized Editorial Design:**
  - Designed with an intentional **monochromatic color palette** (deep zinc neutrals, obsidian tones, crisp hairline borders).
  - Clean typographical branding — no generic AI badges or shield logos.
  - Balanced 2-column hero layout with zero component overlap.
- **Explainable Verification Framework:**
  - Goes beyond crude true/false binaries: breaks down every claim into **Context & Plausibility**, **Evidence Limitations**, and **What the Reader Should Verify**.
  - Provides practical action checklists before forwarding messages into family group chats.
  - Links to verified official portals (e.g., Press Information Bureau Fact Check, Reserve Bank of India, National Portal of India).
- **Interactive Multi-Format Ingestion:**
  - Supports **Text forwards**, **Image screenshots** (local preview), **Document circulars**, and **Web URLs**.
  - Predefined benchmark claims for instant 1-click testing.
- **Multilingual Experience:**
  - Switch between **8 Indian languages**: English, Hindi (हिंदी), Marathi (मराठी), Bengali (বাংলা), Tamil (தமிழ்), Telugu (తెలుగు), Gujarati (ગુજરાતી), and Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ).
  - Pre-translated interface labels and reasoning breakdowns for English, Hindi, and Marathi.
- **Local Persistence & History:**
  - Interactive **Recent Checks** screen with search, status filtering, and LocalStorage caching.
  - 1-click clipboard summary export for forwarding verified debunks.
